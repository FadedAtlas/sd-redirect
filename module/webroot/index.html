Per-app mounts the SD folders into each app process when it starts. It touches nothing global, but the app may look at its folders a few milliseconds before the mount is in place.

Global mounts once at boot for the whole system, so other apps such as the Play Store see the redirect too. No race, but experimental.

Troubleshooting

Log shows what the module did: redirects, blocked starts, moves and errors. Diagnostics collects device info, storage mounts and the config; send it along when reporting a problem.

Module data

Undoes every redirect and deletes the app list, pinned SD cards, mode and log, like a fresh install. No app data is deleted.
